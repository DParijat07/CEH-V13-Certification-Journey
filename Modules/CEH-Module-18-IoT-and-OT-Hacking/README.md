# Module 18 – IoT and OT Hacking

## Overview

The Internet of Things (IoT) and Operational Technology (OT) have transformed industries by connecting physical devices, industrial equipment, and critical infrastructure to modern networks. While these technologies improve automation, efficiency, and remote management, they also introduce new cybersecurity risks.

This module introduces the fundamentals of IoT and OT environments, their architectures, communication protocols, common threats, and modern security controls used to protect connected devices and industrial systems.

The focus is on understanding how IoT and OT systems operate, identifying security risks, and implementing defensive strategies suitable for enterprise and industrial environments.

---

# Learning Objectives

After completing this module, you should be able to:

- Explain the fundamentals of IoT and OT.
- Identify major IoT components and architectures.
- Understand Industrial Control Systems (ICS).
- Describe SCADA, PLC, DCS, RTU, and HMI.
- Compare Information Technology (IT) and Operational Technology (OT).
- Understand common IoT and OT communication protocols.
- Identify common IoT and industrial cyber threats.
- Explain security controls used in IoT and OT environments.
- Understand MITRE ATT&CK for ICS.
- Apply Blue Team monitoring concepts to industrial environments.
