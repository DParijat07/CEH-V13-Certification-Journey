# Module 09 — Social Engineering

This directory contains practical exercises related to **Social Engineering** from CEH v13.

The focus is on understanding human-centered security risks, recognizing social engineering techniques, evaluating awareness controls, and practicing defensive measures in safe, authorized environments.

## Practical Areas

- Social engineering principles and attack vectors
- Phishing awareness and email analysis
- Spear-phishing indicators
- Email spoofing concepts and detection
- Pretexting and impersonation scenarios
- Baiting and removable-media risks
- Social engineering reconnaissance
- Human-factor security assessment
- Security awareness testing
- Incident reporting and prevention

## Tools and Resources

- Isolated training environments

## Practical Workflow

**Define Objective → Prepare Safe Scenario → Identify Indicators → Analyze Risk → Evaluate Controls → Document Findings → Recommend Improvements**

## Evidence and Documentation

Each exercise should document, where applicable:

- Objective and authorized scope
- Scenario description
- Tools and methods used
- Screenshots or sanitized sample evidence
- Suspicious indicators identified
- Potential impact
- Awareness and prevention recommendations
- Lessons learned

## Authorization and Safety

Practical activities must use authorized simulations, synthetic data, or approved training platforms. Do not impersonate real people, collect actual credentials, send deceptive messages without explicit approval, or target individuals without authorization.

## Learning Outcome

The objective is to recognize social engineering risks, analyze suspicious communications, understand human-centered security weaknesses, and recommend effective awareness and preventive controls.
